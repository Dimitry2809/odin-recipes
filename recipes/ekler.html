Choux pastries

History of origin

It all began in France, at the court of Catherine de' Medici. The court pastry chef prepared the dough in a saucepan over the fire. He formed it into flat cakes and placed them in the oven. As they puffed up, a hollow space formed inside; They put pear jam in there and got a sweet dessert.

It took the French centuries to come up with the idea of ​​cooking a mixture of eggs and flour in milk to create "choux pastry" (or "cooked dough"); around the same time, they decided to shape this dough into elongated sticks, which they dubbed "lightning bolts" (*éclairs*).
